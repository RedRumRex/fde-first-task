# Tone Guide (placeholder)

> This is a placeholder until the customer's real tone guide arrives. To use theirs, swap in their file; the drafter reads this file on every run.

**Voice:** clear, confident and plain-spoken, like a smart colleague explaining the news over coffee. We're not a press release, and we're not a hot take.

**Audience:** technical professionals and builders who are short on time.

## Rules
1. **Lead with the news.** The first paragraph says what happened, who did it and when.
2. **Short sentences.** Aim for under 25 words, one idea each.
3. **Facts are always cited.** Every number, date, name or event gets a source. If you can't cite it, cut it.
4. **Opinion is labelled.** Use phrases like "For builders, this means…" or "The open question is…". Opinion sentences never introduce new facts or numbers.
5. **No hype words:** "revolutionary," "game-changing," "unprecedented" (unless a source says it and you're quoting them), "in today's fast-paced world."
6. **Numbers stay as the source wrote them.** Don't convert currencies, round, or calculate new figures.
7. **Be honest when sources disagree.** Name the conflict and don't choose a side quietly.
8. **Structure:** a headline (under 12 words), 3 to 5 sections with short headings, then a "Why it matters" section at the end.
9. **Length:** 350 to 500 words.
10. **No emojis, no exclamation marks.**
